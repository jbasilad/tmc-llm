Dear Students,
It is with great pleasure and enthusiasm that I welcome you to our esteemed institution, the
Trinidad Municipal College (TMC) and present to you the Student Manual. As the Chairman
of the Board of Trustees of this institution, I am honored to be a part of your educational
journey, and I believe that this manual will play a pivotal role in your academic and personal
growth.
This manual has been thoughtfully crafted to serve as your guide, offering valuable
information and resources to help you navigate through your time here. Whether you are a
new or returning student, this manual is designed to empower you with the knowledge
necessary to make the most of your experience.
Within these pages, you will find essential information about the various academic
programs, campus facilities, student services, and the policies that govern our institution.
Additionally, the manual provides insights into the values and principles that underpin our
educational philosophy, emphasizing the importance of integrity, responsibility, and respect
in fostering a nurturing and inclusive learning environment.

Remember, your time as a student is a precious chapter in your life, and I implore you to
make the most of it. Engage with your studies, participate in extracurricular activities, and
forge meaningful connections with your peers and mentors. Your contributions and unique
perspectives are invaluable assets that enrich our academic community.
As you navigate through the challenges and triumphs of your academic journey, always
remember that you are not alone. Our institution is here to support you every step of the
way, and I encourage you to seek help whenever needed. Your success is our success, and
we take immense pride in witnessing you thrive and accomplish your dreams.
In conclusion, I extend my warmest wishes to each one of you. Embrace the opportunities
