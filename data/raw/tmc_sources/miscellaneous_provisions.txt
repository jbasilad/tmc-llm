ARTICLE VI MISCELLANEOUS AND FINAL PROVISIONS:

ARTICLE vI
MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS:
The TMC Board of Trustees pursuant to Section 38, (c), Article 3 of RA 9165 otherwise
known as of “Dangerous Drug Act of 2002” and also CHED Memorandum Order 18 Series
2018, reserves the right to require faculty, staff and students to undergo a random drug test
when, in their assessment, circumstances demand for such drug testing.
ARTICLE vII
FINAL PROVISIONS:
SECTION 1. This Student Manual shall govern all dispositions and
conduct of the   students in TMC.
SECTION 2.    Any doubt in the interpretation of the provisions of
Student Manual shall be resolved first in favor of the College  and
second to the students.
SECTION 3.  Any amendment to or revision of this student
manual may be proposed by the:
a. Local Chief Executive
b. Local Legislative Council
c. Board of Trustees
d. College President
e. Deans and Heads
f. TMC Faculty and Staff
SECTION 4.  Any amendment to, or revision of this Student
Manual shall be valid when   approved by the Board of Trustees,
then adopted by the  Sangguniang Bayan and finally approved
and signed by the Local Chief  Executive of the LGU of Trinidad,
Bohol.
SECTION 5. Any existing laws, memoranda, administrative
announcements or letters of instruction involving the student’s
disposition and conduct in the College not inconsistent with this
Student Manual shall remain effective until amended, repealed or
revoked.
SECTION 6. This Student Manual shall take effect immediately
upon the approval of the Board of Trustees, adopted by the
Sangguniang Bayan and approved by Local Chief Executive of the
LGU of Trinidad.
